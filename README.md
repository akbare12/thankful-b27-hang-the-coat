A short thank-you for the judge who carried a heavy coat through a long day of reading. One small act: take the coat from tired shoulders, shake off the dust, hang it by the door, and let it rest overnight — so the coat, and the judge, can both breathe again.

## Entry text (327 characters)

> Thank you. Your coat has carried the weight of every verdict you weighed today, heavy on your shoulders all day. I take it from you now, shake the dust from its shoulders, and hang it on the peg by the door. Stand a little taller without it. Tonight it rests there, empty sleeves still, waiting to keep you warm again tomorrow.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes this exact text to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
